Have you ever wanted something different out of the Brain Imaging Data Structure (BIDS) standard? Yarik Halchenko (of the Center for Open Neuroscience) will give the NIMH Data Science & Sharing Team's Lunch & Learn series a half-hour talk on the future of BIDS with a focus on the in-development BIDS 2.0, the first backwards-incompatible revision of the standard. This presentation and discussion will be especially interesting to neuroimaging researchers, dataset curators, and developers of BIDS-aware tools. Attendees can expect an overview of the overarching agenda guiding BIDS 2.0 together with a tour of how the work is organized across the bids-2-devel discussion repository, plus pointers on how to weigh in or contribute.
