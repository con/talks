🚀 Call for Submissions: USRSE’26 – Advancing Science in the Age of AI

The USRSE’26 conference is now accepting submissions for its in-person program, centered around the theme “Advancing Science in the Age of AI: Explore how RSEs and collaborators increase research impact during these rapidly changing times.” Whether you are showcasing original research, sharing reproducible workflows, or facilitating community discussions, there is a track designed for your contribution.

Submission Tracks Include:

Papers & Talks: Original work and broader topics of interest to the RSE community.
Notebooks: Demonstrating reproducible computational workflows.
Workshops & Birds of a Feather: Teaching new skills or discussing shared interests.
Posters: Engaging with the community during the dynamic poster reception.
Key Details:

Theme: “Advancing Science in the Age of AI: Explore how RSEs and collaborators increase research impact during these rapidly changing times.”
AI Policy: Submissions utilizing Generative AI/LLMs must adhere to IEEE guidelines on AI-generated text.
Review Process: All submissions undergo peer review for quality, relevance, and novelty.
Deadline & Portal: Submit via the USRSE’26 EasyChair site: https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=usrse26
For full details on track requirements and participation, visit: https://us-rse.org/usrse26/participate/
