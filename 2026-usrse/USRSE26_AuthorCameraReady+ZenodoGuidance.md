# USRSE’26 Camera Ready Guidance

&nbsp;

Thank you to all authors who have already completed the tasks in the camera ready guidance that was sent last month. If you have not done so already, please note that there are deadlines associated with all tracks coming up this week. The full camera ready guidance is reproduced below for your convenience:&nbsp;

&nbsp;

---

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

Congratulations on your accepted submission. As you revise your work and put together your presentation for the conference, please consider the reviewer feedback when preparing your final camera-ready version but use your best judgement when making changes. We request that you upload slightly different artifacts depending on the track that your submission was accepted in.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

**For all tracks please take care to ensure that the final submission title, author list, and author order are as you would like them to appear in the conference program.**

&nbsp;

If you have any questions please do not hesitate to contact your Track Chairs by replying to the email about these logistics or to the Technical Program Co-Chairs directly at usrse26-tech-committee@us-rse.org.

&nbsp;

For questions related to the Zenodo submission, please contact the Publications Co-Chairs at usrse26-publications@us-rse.org.

&nbsp;

Below you will find guidelines for contributing your work to the conference’s Zenodo community, and some guidance for particular sub-tracks as well.

# **Track Specific Guidelines**

## 

## **Papers**

* Please ensure formatting to IEEE’s Manuscript template  
  * [https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates)&nbsp;  
* Revise your paper based on reviewer feedback and the meta commentary from the Papers Co-Chairs.  
* Send your camera ready draft to the Papers Co-Chairs for final sign off and address any final comments they may have.

&nbsp;

***Journal Special Issue —*** We are working on a special issue, details should be announced before the conference.

## 

## **Talks**

* Please ensure your abstract PDF has removed unused template sections and information.

&nbsp;

## **Notebooks**

* Revise your notebook repository as needed based on reviews or any updates since submission. We request that these be minimal if possible.&nbsp;  
* We suggest creating a tagged release in the repo labeled “usrse26” or something similar.  
* For Zenodo, prepare a rendered HTML version of the notebook, and optionally a PDF

&nbsp;

## **Posters**

* Please upload a PDF of your abstract by the first date.  
* A PDF of your poster itself is not due until the conference timeframe.

# **Zenodo Submission Instructions**

Deadlines to submit artifacts to Zenodo are below for each track. We encourage you to upload a PDF of your presentation shortly before or during the conference for interested attendees.

&nbsp;

**Clarification on Sept. 11: We are requesting Talk and Poster abstracts be uploaded to Zenodo by September 16th so that we can add the entry’s DOI to the conference program on the website. This way readers will be able to easily access your materials when they are uploaded as a revision at the later date.**

&nbsp;

***All deadlines are 11:59pm (anywhere on Earth).***

&nbsp;

* Talks, Posters  
  * Abstracts — **Wednesday September 16th**  
  * Add Talk slides or PDF of Poster to the Zenodo no later than — **Wednesday October 21st**  
* Notebooks  
  * Notebook file(s) — **Wednesday September 16th**  
  * Add Talk slides to the Zenodo entry no later than — **Wednesday October 21st**  
* Papers  
  * Revised, camera ready draft sent to Papers Co-Chairs — **Wednesday September 16th**  
  * Upload to Zenodo — **Friday September 25th**  
  * Add Talk slides to the Zenodo entry no later than — **Wednesday October 21st**

&nbsp;

**Correction on Sept. 11: The Poster acceptance email stated Camera Ready abstracts should be uploaded by October 18th. This was a typo and should have read September 16th. However, given our error Poster authors are asked to upload at their convenience.**

## **Basic information**

![][image3]

&nbsp;

* Digital Object Identifier *\[required\]*  
  * Select “No, I need one”  
  * Optionally click “Get a DOI now\!” to obtain immediately, otherwise it will be generated when you submit for publication.  
* Resource type *\[required\]*  
  * Select as appropriate for your track  
    * Papers — "Publication / Conference paper"  
    * Talks — “Presentation”  
    * Notebooks — "Software / Computational notebook"  
    * Posters — "Poster"  
* Title *\[required\]*  
  * Use the title submitted to the USRSE'26 Conference  
* Publication date *\[required\]*  
  * Please set this to “2026-10-19”  
* Authors/Creators *\[required\]*  
  * Click "Add creator" and follow instructions on the dialog box.  
  * Please add the **ORCID** of all authors, if available.  
* Description *\[required\]*  
  * Use the abstract submitted to the USRSE'25 Conference.  
  * This may be a duplicate with your PDF. This is okay.  
* License *\[required\]*  
  * Leave default, "**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**" will be used.
