Third-Party Logos and Trademarks
================================

This is NOT a license grant. It is a marker.

Files annotated with `LicenseRef-ThirdPartyLogo` are logos, wordmarks,
banners and other trademarks belonging to third-party organisations:
funding agencies, institutions, collaborating projects and software
projects.

They are reproduced in the slides of this repository for nominative
use only -- that is, to identify and credit the funders, institutions
and projects being named on a given slide. This is the ordinary
practice of acknowledgement slides in academic presentations.

    where they are still accurate acknowledgements, and consult the
    owner's brand/trademark guidelines before any other use.
