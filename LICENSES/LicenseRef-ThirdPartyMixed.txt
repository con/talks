Third-Party Material of Mixed or Undetermined Provenance
========================================================

This is NOT a license grant. It is a marker.

Files annotated with `LicenseRef-ThirdPartyMixed` are third-party
material that was collected into this talks repository over many years
for use as illustrations inside presentation slides: screenshots of
web pages and publications, figures from papers and posters, cartoons,
photographs, and similar.

For these files:

  * The copyright is held by the respective original authors,
    publishers or rights holders -- NOT by the maintainers of this
    repository.

    One exception, marked as such where it applies: a few files are our
    own compositions built on top of borrowed pieces (a diagram whose
    layers include third-party clipart, a figure embedding a borrowed
    photograph).  There the annotation names us as holder of the
    composition AND records undetermined third-party rights in the
    embedded elements.  The whole still cannot be offered under this
    repository's terms alone, which is why it carries this marker.

  * They are NOT licensed under this repository's CC-BY-SA-4.0 terms.
    The repository-wide license applies to the slides, diagrams, text
    and other material authored here, and cannot and does not extend
    to third-party works merely reproduced within them.

  * Their individual provenance and licensing terms have not been
    determined, and in many cases can no longer be reconstructed. The
    `SPDX-FileCopyrightText` for these files is recorded as
    `NOASSERTION` for exactly that reason: it asserts ignorance rather
    than ownership.

  * Their inclusion here is as quotation/illustration in an academic
    presentation context. Reproducing them outside that context, or
    redistributing them on the strength of this repository's license,
    is NOT authorised by this repository. If you wish to reuse one of
    these files, identify and contact the original rights holder.

Where provenance IS known for a particular file or group of files, a
*later* annotation block in `REUSE.toml` overrides this marker and
records the actual holder and license.  Position in the file is what
decides, not how narrow the pattern is: only the last matching block is
ever consulted. Such corrections are welcome:
please open an issue or a pull request.
